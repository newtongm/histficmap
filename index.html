Covers shown here are placeholder dispatch cards, not real cover art. Coastlines are simplified (Natural Earth, public domain) and are not political borders, since national borders shift over time in ways that would misrepresent historical settings. Only books within about 10 years of the selected date are shown on the map at all — check "Show all" to ignore the timeline and see everything at once. Nearby pins merge into numbered clusters; click one to fan its members out around a central point (click the center to collapse it again). A dashed trail means the story moves between places; its pin marks where it starts. Pin colors show a book's genre and double as the Genre filter. The Series, Genre, Topic and Author filters only list what is on the map at the selected date and view.
